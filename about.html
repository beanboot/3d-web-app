3D Garden Web App

Take good care of your plant and relax in this 3D web application.

Main Features

Plant Health

The plant has an internal water level that drains over time. Watering the plant increases its water level. The water level controls the size and colour of the plant.

Day/Night Cycle

The sun and moon rotate around the scene on a shared axis, with the sun's position determining the time of day. The sun's height is used to linearly interpolate sky colours and star visibility.

Clouds and Stars

Clouds are built in Three.js by grouping multiple spheres into a single object. Each cloud orbits the scene at a different radius and height, interleaved so they feel naturally spread across the sky. Stars are generated by distributing 500 points randomly across a large sphere surrounding the scene using spherical coordinates, rendered as a point cloud.

A Development Challenge

Unused Growth Animation

The original growing animation was made in Blender using curve and deform modifiers. I was unable to export the animation as the GLTF format doesn't support modifier animation, and baking the frames proved difficult.

Built with Bootstrap, Three.js, and Blender for a coursework project at the University of Sussex.
